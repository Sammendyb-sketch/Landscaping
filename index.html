About Verdant & Co.

14 Years of Excellence. We don't just plant gardens. We craft legacies. Founded in 2010 by master horticulturist Elena Marsh, Verdant & Co. has grown into the Pacific Northwest's most celebrated landscape design studio. Our team of 28 specialists brings together artistry, ecology, and precision craftsmanship. Every project begins with a conversation — about your vision, your lifestyle, your land. We listen deeply before we draw a single line.

  • Award-winning design recognized by the ASLA Pacific Northwest Chapter
  • Sustainable, native plant-forward approach to every landscape
  • Full-service from concept through maintenance — one relationship, one team
  • Licensed, bonded, and insured with a 5-year craftsmanship guarantee

What We Do / Our Services

From intimate garden redesigns to sweeping estate transformations — we bring the full spectrum of landscape artistry to your property.

01 🌿

Landscape Design

Comprehensive design plans drawn by our licensed landscape architects — from planting schedules to 3D visualizations of your future garden.

02 🪨

Hardscape & Stonework

Patios, pathways, retaining walls, pergolas, and outdoor kitchens — crafted with natural stone, premium pavers, and hand-selected materials.

03 💧

Water Features

Custom ponds, waterfalls, reflecting pools, and fountain systems designed to bring the calming presence of water to your property.

04 💡

Landscape Lighting

Architectural and accent lighting systems that extend the beauty of your landscape into the evening hours, safely and dramatically.

05 🌱

Ongoing Maintenance

Seasonal care plans, lawn programs, pruning, fertilization, and irrigation management — keeping your landscape pristine year-round.

06 🌀

Smart Irrigation

Precision irrigation systems with smart controllers, rain sensors, and zone management — saving water while keeping everything perfectly hydrated.

How It Works / Our Process

A seamless journey from first conversation to finished masterpiece — every step designed around your vision and comfort.

01

Free Discovery Call

We start with a 30-minute conversation to understand your vision, budget, and timeline. No pressure, no commitment — just exploration.

02

On-Site Assessment

Our lead designer visits your property to assess soil, drainage, sun exposure, and existing plants — the foundation of any great design.

03

Design Presentation

We present a full design package including concept renderings, plant lists, material samples, and itemized pricing — bringing your vision to life on paper.

04

Build & Installation

Our expert crews execute the design with precision and care, communicating with you throughout and minimizing disruption to your daily life.

05

Ongoing Care Plan

We don't disappear after installation — choose from our maintenance plans to keep your landscape thriving through every season, year after year.
